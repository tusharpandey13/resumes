---
purpose: Follow-up message after submitting an application — surfaces interest, prompts next step
when-to-use: 5–7 days after applying with no response; also adaptable as a reply to a recruiter who has reached out
limit: ~300 words; short enough to read at a glance
variables:
  - "{{company}}"       — company display name
  - "{{role}}"          — exact role title from JD
  - "{{contact_name}}"  — first name of recruiter or contact (use "there" if unknown)
  - "{{applied_date}}"  — date application was submitted (YYYY-MM-DD or natural language)
  - "{{shared_context}}"— 1-line hook from analysis.md (why this role, what you bring)
---
